Long-Haired German Shepherd

The long-haired German Shepherd is a variety of the classic German Shepherd, but with long, silky hair.
Personally, I like the breed with a rich black coat and red or golden highlights.

Intelligence and character

The long-haired German Shepherd is an intelligent, loyal, and well-balanced dog. It is highly intelligent and easy to train (it learns commands quickly)
and has a well-developed protective instinct. The Shepherd has a good guarding instinct (wary of strangers and determined to protect its owner) but at the
same time is kind to children and family members. This breed of dog is very emotionally attached to people and needs attention, warmth, and a stable environment.
It does not like to be left alone for long periods of time.

Behavior

Long-haired sheepdogs are energetic, active dogs that need physical and mental stimulation. They require:

  • daily walks;
  • outdoor games;
  • training and commands;
  • the owner's attention;

If such a dog is left without attention, exercise, and training, behavioral problems may arise.

Interesting facts:

  • until 2010, long-haired sheepdogs were not recognized as standard. They were considered a “deviation” from the breed, and puppies with long hair were not allowed to breed.
    was only in 2010 that the International Cynological Federation (FCI) recognized long-haired sheepdogs as an official variety of German shepherd link;
  • life expectancy: 9–13 years;
  • temperature sensitivity: very thick, long hair can make them more sensitive to hot weather than short-haired dogs;
  • Long hair gene. Long hair only appears when both parents carry the corresponding recessive gene. Therefore, fewer such puppies
    are born than short-haired ones link.
